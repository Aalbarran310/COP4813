Prerequisite and course-choice notes

  • AND: all courses listed in allOf must be completed.
  • OR: at least one course listed in anyOf must be completed.
  • C# / Java choice: COP2360 or COP2800 may be selected for the program choice, not both.
  • Electives: the planner limits the student to three planned program electives.

The OR-prerequisite example is a practice demonstration only, not an actual Daytona State course prerequisite. COP2360 is shown with COP1000 as its catalog prerequisite; verify all current requirements in the official catalog.

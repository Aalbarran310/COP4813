RC Circuit Capacitor Charging

This calculator demonstrates the voltage across a capacitor as it charges in an RC (resistor-capacitor) circuit. The equation is nonlinear and shows how the capacitor approaches the source voltage over time.

V(t) = V₀(1 − e−t/(RC))

Enter the circuit values below and select a time range. The calculator will calculate the capacitor voltage and create a plot with time on the X-axis and voltage on the Y-axis.

RC Circuit Calculator

This calculator uses a nonlinear RC capacitor-charging equation and plots capacitor voltage over time.

Equation

The calculator uses the capacitor charging equation below. The source voltage, resistance, capacitance, and time range can be changed to create a new plot.

V(t) = V₀(1 − e−t/(RC))

The independent variable, time, is plotted on the X-axis. The dependent variable, capacitor voltage, is plotted on the Y-axis.

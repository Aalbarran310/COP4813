What I found

WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla are open-source systems that can be installed with a hosting provider. They offer flexibility through themes and extensions, but the site owner is responsible for hosting, updates, and security maintenance.

Wix and Shopify are hosted services. They handle much of the server maintenance, which can make them easier to start using. The trade-off is that the site depends more on the provider's platform and rules. Wix is aimed at a wide range of websites, while Shopify is mainly focused on online stores.

Each system has a different purpose. The best choice depends on the type of website, the amount of customization needed, the available budget, and how much technical maintenance the owner wants to handle.
